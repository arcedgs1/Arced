## Remaining limits

Workers now have a limited My Time interface; clients still do not have a portal. Time corrections/rejection/formal submission, payroll exports, employee password change/reset, daily-rate/burden calculations, equipment photos/purchase/warranty fields and incident attachments, overhead receipt uploads, offsite scheduled backups, Stripe, email/SMS, client portal, and specialized division workflows remain unfinished.

Team and operations endpoints are restricted to owner/admin/office manager; workers receive only their own time and assigned job titles, without rate/cost data. Manual labor entries are not automatically matched to approved shifts: do not record the same labor twice. All hosting charges, resource creation, live HTTPS, and a real employee-phone trial remain to be completed in Render. The hosted database starts clean and does not automatically import the Windows installation's data. See RENDER-DEPLOY.md and REQUIREMENTS.md.

Branding remains based on checked-in styles because live-site retrieval was denied during initial setup. No production-readiness, publication, live-provider-payment or comprehensive security claim is made.
